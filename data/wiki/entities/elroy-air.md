# Elroy Air

California-based heavy-lift drone developer awarded $46M multi-year contract by US Army for autonomous hybrid-electric VTOL cargo drone development.

## Overview

Elroy Air develops autonomous hybrid-electric VTOL (vertical take-off and landing) cargo drones designed for military logistics in challenging operating environments.

## US Army Contract

- **Amount**: $46 million multi-year contract
- **Purpose**: Further development of autonomous hybrid-electric VTOL cargo drone
- **Goal**: Enhanced capability for supporting troops in toughest operating environments

## Technology

- Hybrid-electric VTOL design
- Autonomous operation
- Heavy-lift cargo capability
- Designed for military logistics applications
