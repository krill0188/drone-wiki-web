# FlybyOps

FlybyOps is a Finland-based enterprise drone operations platform company. Launched in August 2026, their platform is designed for large-scale Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) and autonomous drone programs.

## Platform Capabilities

The enterprise platform addresses challenges as drone programs grow in size and complexity:

- **Compliance management** — regulatory adherence at scale
- **Risk management** — operational risk assessment
- **Operational oversight** — fleet management and monitoring

## Target Market

Commercial drone operators scaling from small VLOS operations to enterprise BVLOS programs requiring:
- Multi-aircraft coordination
- Regulatory compliance automation
- Mission planning and execution
- Safety management systems
