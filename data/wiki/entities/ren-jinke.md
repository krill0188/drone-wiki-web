# Ren, Jinke

Ren, Jinke is an author of the paper *"Fluid Antennas Enabled UAV Communication Systems: Fundamentals, Challenges, and Future Directions"* (2026), which investigates low-altitude wireless networks (LAWNs) using fluid antenna (FA) technology for UAV communication systems.

## 개요

Fluid antennas (FAs) provide an additional degree of freedom (DoF) by reconfiguring antenna positions within a compact aperture. In this work, Ren, Jinke contributes to the investigation of FA-enabled UAV communication systems from perspectives of fundamentals, design challenges, and future research directions. The paper introduces a dual-mobility perspective where UAV trajectory planning provides macro-mobility for large-scale air-to-ground geometry adaptation, and FA position reconfiguration provides micro-mobility for fine-grained local channel adaptation.
