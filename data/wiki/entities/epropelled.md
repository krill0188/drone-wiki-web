# ePropelled

ePropelled is a U.S. manufacturer of advanced electric propulsion systems for uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs). In August 2026, the company announced a major expansion supported by $60 million in U.S. government funding.

## Expansion Details

- **Location**: New Hampshire manufacturing facility
- **Funding**: $60 million U.S. government support
- **Capacity increase**: Production footprint quadrupled
- **Purpose**: Strengthen domestic drone propulsion supply chain

## Strategic Significance

The expansion addresses U.S. policy priorities for:
- Domestic drone component manufacturing
- Reducing reliance on foreign propulsion systems
- Supporting defense and commercial UAV markets

## Product Focus

Advanced electric propulsion systems for:
- Uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs)
- Aerospace applications
