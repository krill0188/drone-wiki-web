# Airwise Nexus

Airwise Nexus is an airspace-awareness and sensor-fusion capability launched by Airwise Solutions in August 2026. It operates within the airwiseOS Operational Intelligence platform.

## Capabilities

Nexus brings data from multiple airspace sensors and operational systems into one common operating picture:

- **Radar** — primary surveillance
- **RF detection** — radio frequency sensing
- **Remote ID** — broadcast drone identification
- **ADS-B** — aircraft transponder data
- **Other operational data** — integrated feeds

## Open Integration Philosophy

Airwise Solutions promotes "free and open" sensor integration, allowing operators to:
- Choose best-of-breed sensors
- Avoid vendor lock-in
- Customize their airspace awareness stack

## Use Cases

- UTM (UAS Traffic Management) operations
- BVLOS mission support
- Critical infrastructure protection
- Public safety drone operations
