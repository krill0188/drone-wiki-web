# Wei Zhou

Wei Zhou is a researcher associated with Nanjing University of Aeronautics, contributing to the field of AI-driven UAV swarm trajectory planning in complex dynamic environments.

## 개요
The document introduces Wei Zhou as an author of a 2026 paper presenting a novel phased trajectory planning method for UAV swarm cooperation. The work proposes a two-phase approach—global planning using an improved genetic algorithm with multi-objective optimization, and online local replanning using a hierarchical strategy with a variable-step sparse A* algorithm—to optimize trajectory length, smoothness, and reduce three-dimensional search complexity in complex dynamic environments.
