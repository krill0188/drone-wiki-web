# RadioMaster GX15

The RadioMaster GX15 is an ELRS 2.4GHz radio controller released in August 2026, positioned as a mid-size option between the compact GX12 and the larger TX16s Mk3.

## Specifications

- **Protocol**: ELRS 2.4GHz
- **Size**: Mid-size (between GX12 and TX16s Mk3)
- **Intended use**: FPV drone control

## Reviews

Multiple reviewers noted the GX15 hits a "sweet spot" for size — larger than the GX12 for better ergonomics, but more portable than full-size transmitters like the TX16s Mk3.
