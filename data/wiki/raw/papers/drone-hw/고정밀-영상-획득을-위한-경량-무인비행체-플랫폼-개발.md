## Abstract

This study presents the development of a lightweight unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) system optimized for high-precision image acquisition and efficient field operability. To enhance mobility and operational flexibility, the UAV was miniaturized and integrated with a mission computer, camera control systems, and LTE-based communication modules within a compact form factor. The design includes a lightweight airframe and a self-developed integrated hybrid board combining the flight controller (FC), electronic speed controller (ESC), and power distribution board (PDB) into a single unit, reducing total weight while maintaining flight stability and endurance.
