## Abstract

This study proposes a cost-minimizing optimization framework for urban drone-human hybrid delivery. We formulate a mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) model that jointly determines hub and charging-station locations, the configuration of the drone network among installed facilities, and the delivery mode assigned to each customer. To better reflect operating conditions, the model incorporates terrain-related flight restrictions, terrain-dependent cost weights, coverage limits, and payload constraints.
