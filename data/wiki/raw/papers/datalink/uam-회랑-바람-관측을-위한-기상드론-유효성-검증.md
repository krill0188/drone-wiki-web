## Abstract

Safe urban air mobility (UAM) operations require reliable wind information across flight heights (0~600m Above Ground Level, AGL). This study quantitatively validates drone-based wind observations against a wind profiler (WPF) and proposes a field-operable flight protocol. In addition, power-law vertical wind profiles, commonly used for low-altitude wind estimation, are compared with drone observations to examine their limitations. A total of eight experiments covering 10–500m AGL were conducted at the WPF Seogwipo site (ID: 47884) from August to September 2024.
