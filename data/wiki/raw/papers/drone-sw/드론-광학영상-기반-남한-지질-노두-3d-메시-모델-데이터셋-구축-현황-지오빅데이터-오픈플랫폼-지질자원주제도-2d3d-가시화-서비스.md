## Abstract

This paper describes a dataset of drone-based optical 3D mesh models of geological outcrops across South Korea, developed by the Korea Institute of Geoscience and Mineral Resources (KIGAM). The dataset includes 133 three-dimensional (3D) mesh models representing 80 outcrop groups in nine province-level administrative regions. These models were constructed from optical imagery acquired with various DJI drone platforms since October 2018. The outcrops cover a broad range of geological settings across the country. These include dinosaur track fossil sites and sedimentary strata of the Cretaceous Gyeongsang Basin, Cenozoic volcanic columnar joints and tuffs, mylonite and gneiss of metamorphic complexes, fault exposures, and the landforms of Jeju Island.
