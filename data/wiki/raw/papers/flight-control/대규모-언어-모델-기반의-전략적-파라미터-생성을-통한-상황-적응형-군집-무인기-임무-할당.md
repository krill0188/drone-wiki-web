## Abstract

This paper proposes a novel hybrid framework for multiple Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) task allocation that bridges the semantic gap between strategic intent and numerical control in dynamic environments. Traditional optimization algorithms often fail to adapt flexibly to unpredictable events due to their reliance on rigid, rule-based logic. To address this limitation, we introduce a hierarchical hybrid decision-making architecture that integrates Large Language Model (LLM) with the Consensus-Based Bundle Algorithm (CBBA). In this framework, the LLM serves as a strategic planner, interpreting the semantic context of dynamic events and generating high-level strategic parameters,  which  then  guide  the  CBBA’s  decentralized  execution.
