# A Novel Efficient Particle Swarm Optimization Algorithm for UAV Applications in Bushfire Hazard Management

## Abstract

Abstract
                  Owing to the drastic environmental change and frequently occurring disasters like bushfires in Australia and New South Wales (NSW) in particular, this research study aims to develop an unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) assisted bushfire detection and prevention system. A rule-based verification framework is developed to identify critical infrastructure impacted by bushfires using a metaheuristic optimization algorithm, with reliance on real-time data. Moreover, the affected area coverage of UAVs is maximized by considering limited resources and energy constraints for better precision of results.
