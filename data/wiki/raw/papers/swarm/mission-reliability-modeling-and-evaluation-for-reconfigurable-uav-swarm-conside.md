## Abstract

This article presents a mission reliability modeling and evaluation framework for reconfigurable UAV swarms executing phased missions, wherein node failure rates vary across phases due to the changing operational conditions and stresses. The proposed coordinated framework integrates dynamic reconfiguration and mission abort decisions. Specifically, a recon-figuration strategy restores effective ‘detect-decide-engage’ loops via node replacement and linkreorganization. Meanwhile, a mission abort policy could be triggered by the real-time effective target coverage ratio. The phase-specific abort thresholds are optimized using a simulated annealing algorithm to balance mission success probability and resource conservation.
