## Abstract

Covert spoofing technology, which injects counterfeit signals into Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)/ Inertial Navigation System (INS) integrated navigation systems to hijack and redirect a target vehicle to a desired target, recently has been gaining significant attention as a means of countering the threat of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV). However, existing spoofing techniques require prior knowledge of the UAV's internal navigation parameters or flight trajectory. In practical scenarios, such internal information is strictly inaccessible to the spoofer. To address this issue, this paper presents a covert spoofing framework that employs a dual-concealment reward in Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), a maximum entropy reinforcement learning algorithm.
