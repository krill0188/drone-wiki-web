## Abstract

In wireless mesh networks (WMNs), multi-hop transmissions, dynamic topologies, and shared channels contribute to intense inter-node resource competition. This competition becomes particularly severe under high node density or bursty traffic conditions, often resulting in network congestion and performance degradation. To address this challenge, this paper proposes a blocking-aware multi-dimensional priority optimization (BMPO) strategy for bandwidth allocation in unmanned aerial vehicle - visible light communication (UAV-VLC) assisted WMNs. The BMPO framework establishes a three-dimensional node state perception model by integrating real-time channel utilization, interference prediction via a hidden Markov model, and probabilistic congestion risk derived from queuing theory.
