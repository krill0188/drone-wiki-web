## Abstract

Plant construction projects involve complex logistics of material procurement, transportation, and installation, making real-time tracking of material location and quantity a critical challenge in construction management. As Advanced Work Packaging(AWP) is increasingly adopted, the need to link material information with work packages has grown substantially. However, material tracking at plant construction sites still relies heavily on manual inspection, limiting the accuracy and timeliness of information. This study designs an information system for drone-based image processing to support automated material tracking at plant construction sites, and concretizes the system through a database schema and Entity Relationship Diagram(ERD).
