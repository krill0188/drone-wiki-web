# 야간 해양 수색용 드론의 해무 대응 적응형 조명 운용 방안

**Authors**: 강혜란(세종대학교),   
**Year**: 2026  
**URL**: https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART003396250

## Abstract

In this study, an adaptive lighting operation strategy for UAV-based nighttime maritime search and rescue under sea fog conditions was proposed based on the operational stages of the IAMSAR framework. The proposed strategy consists of environmental condition assessment, search, identification, and rescue support stages. Visible (VIS) and 850 nm near-infrared (NIR) lighting are selectively employed according to the operational stage and environmental conditions. The lighting beam angle is determined considering the camera field of view, target distance, and required illumination area, while the luminous flux or radiant output is estimated considering atmospheric attenuation.
