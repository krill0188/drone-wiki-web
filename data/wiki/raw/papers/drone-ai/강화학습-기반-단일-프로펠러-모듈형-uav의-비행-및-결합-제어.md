## Abstract

Unmanned vehicles are mobile platforms capable of autonomously performing missions across diverse domains, including ground, maritime, and aerial environments, by leveraging artificial intelligence without direct human intervention. This study proposes a cube-shaped modular unmanned aerial vehicle in which each unit consists of a single propeller and four vanes and can couple with other units in mid-air. Control policies were trained through reinforcement learning in NVIDIA Isaac Lab, a physics-based simulation environment.
