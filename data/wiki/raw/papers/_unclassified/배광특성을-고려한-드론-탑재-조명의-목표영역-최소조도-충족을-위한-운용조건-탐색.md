## Abstract

An operating-condition search was developed to identify drone-mounted lighting conditions satisfying target-area minimum illuminance while accounting for photometric distribution. Using manufacturer-provided IES photometric data, illuminance was calculated for candidate heights and optical-axis orientations, and the required relative light output was determined.
Feasible candidates were ranked by relative light output and the fraction of the area meeting the requirement outside the target. To assess numerical agreement of the illuminance calculation used in the search, results for one dataset were compared with ReluxDesktop, yielding RMSEs of 0.2133–0.2549lx.
