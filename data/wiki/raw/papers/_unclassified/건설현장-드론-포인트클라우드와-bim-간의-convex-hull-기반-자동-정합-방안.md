## Abstract

This study proposes a Convex Hull-based automatic registration method between BIM and drone-based point clouds for construction status analysis. Drone-based point clouds acquired from actual construction sites often contain temporary structures and non-target objects, while interior data may be incomplete due to the limitations of exterior drone imaging. These characteristics create geometric differences between BIM and point cloud data and may reduce the applicability of conventional registration methods that rely on entire shapes or local feature correspondences. To address these limitations, this study uses the lowest floor boundary, which can be commonly identified in both datasets, as the geometric reference for registration.
