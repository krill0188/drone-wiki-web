## Abstract

The integration of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and mobile edge computing (MEC) has emerged as a promising approach for supporting latency-sensitive and computation-intensive services in dynamic wireless environments. In particular, UAV-mounted aerial MEC can reduce communication distance and improve service flexibility, while ground MEC offers more stable and higher computing capacity. This study does not propose a new clustering or offloading algorithm. Instead, it presents a controlled, implementation-oriented comparison of ground-only MEC and hybrid aerial–ground MEC under the same clustered UAV network structure.
