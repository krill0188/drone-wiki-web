# 한반도 백악기 공룡발자국 화석 산지의 드론 정사영상 및 음영기복도 데이터셋

**Authors**: 이홍진(한국지질자원연구원),   
**Year**: 2026  
**URL**: https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART003391953

## Abstract

The Cretaceous Gyeongsang and Neungju basins of southeastern Korea preserve numerous dinosaur track sites, many of them designated cultural heritage, whose exposed track-bearing surfaces are subject to weathering. This dataset provides drone-derived orthomosaic and hillshade imagery for 30 dinosaur track sites (35 mapped areas) in the Gyeongsang and Neungju Basins, supporting the digital recording of these surfaces. The dataset comprises 41 orthomosaics and 41 hillshade images, amounting to 82 georeferenced GeoTIFF files, covering track-bearing strata of the Gyeongsang Supergroup, most commonly the Jindong and Haman formations. Imagery was acquired with several DJI multirotor unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) platforms, using nadir photographs collected along automated flight lines at altitude
