# Drone Coverage Network

Drone coverage network is a system of unmanned aerial vehicles coordinated to provide spatial coverage over a target area, optimized through Bayesian learning methods for specific mission objectives such as emergency response. This technology was applied in a case study regarding cardiac arrest incidents in Scotland.

## 개요

Drone coverage networks enable the systematic surveying and monitoring of geographical areas using multiple drones. Unlike single-drone operations, these networks coordinate multiple aerial units to achieve comprehensive spatial coverage. The defining characteristic of this approach is the integration of Bayesian learning algorithms to optimize flight paths, resource allocation, and coverage efficiency in real-time. The Bayesian framework allows the system to update its understanding of the environment and mission priorities as new data is collected during flight operations.

In the referenced case study, the drone coverage network was specifically applied to the problem of cardiac arrest response in Scotland, demonstrating the technology's potential in emergency medical services and life-critical situations.
