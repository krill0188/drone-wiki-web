# AUVSI Leadership Transition 2026

AUVSI (Association for Uncrewed Vehicle Systems International) announces leadership transition with Michael Robbins departure and Heather Lee appointment as Acting President and CEO.

## Leadership Changes

- **Outgoing**: Michael Robbins (President and CEO)
- **Departure Date**: September 30, 2026
- **Incoming**: Heather Lee (Acting President and CEO)
- **New Role**: Robbins to lead Semiconductor Industry Association

## Transition Details

- AUVSI Board launched search for permanent successor
- Leadership transition effective fall 2026
- Heather Lee currently Chief Operating Officer

## About AUVSI

- Association for Uncrewed Vehicle Systems International
- Leading industry organization for drones and uncrewed systems
- Represents industry interests in policy and regulatory matters
