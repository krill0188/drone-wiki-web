# Uber-Zipline Drone Delivery Partnership

Long-term partnership between Uber and Zipline to bring autonomous drone deliveries to millions of Americans, targeting one million deliveries per day by end of 2029.

## Partnership Overview

- **Companies**: Uber Eats + Zipline
- **Goal**: Autonomous drone deliveries at scale
- **Target**: 1 million deliveries per day by end of 2029
- **Scope**: Millions of Americans

## Strategic Significance

- Major expansion of drone delivery into mainstream consumer market
- Leverages Zipline's autonomous drone delivery expertise
- Integrates with Uber Eats existing delivery infrastructure
