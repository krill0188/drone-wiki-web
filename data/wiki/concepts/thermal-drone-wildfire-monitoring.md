# Thermal Drone Wildfire Monitoring and C-UAS Response

Recent developments in thermal imaging drones for wildfire monitoring and counter-UAS responses to unauthorized drone flights over emergency zones.

## Thermal Imaging for Wildfire Detection

### United Utilities Implementation
- **Location**: Northwest England moorland
- **Purpose**: Locate underground heat pockets that could reignite
- **Support**: Emergency services since June 2026
- **Technology**: Thermal imaging drones

### Capabilities
- Detect hidden hotspots post-fire
- Monitor smoldering areas underground
- Support firefighter safety and resource allocation

## Counter-UAS Response

### Spokane Wildfire Incident
- **Agencies**: FBI and local officials
- **Issue**: Unauthorized drone overflights
- **Impact**: Temporary grounding of firefighting aircraft

### Multi-Agency Task Force Response
- Detection of dozens of unauthorized drones
- Pilot location and identification
- Coordination to maintain firefighting operations
