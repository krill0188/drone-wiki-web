# SmolVLM: Redefining small and efficient multimodal models

- 원문: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05299^[inbox/processed/hw-20261021-model-smolvlm.md]
- 제작: Hugging Face (Andres Marafioti 외 17명)^[inbox/processed/hw-20261021-model-smolvlm.md]

## 핵심 사양 (초록)
- SmolVLM-256M: 'less than 1GB GPU memory during inference', 약 300배 큰 Idefics-80B보다 우수하다고 주장.^[inbox/processed/hw-20261021-model-smolvlm.md]
- SmolVLM-2.2B: 2.2B 파라미터, GPU 메모리를 2배 쓰는 SOTA VLM과 대등하다고 주장.^[inbox/processed/hw-20261021-model-smolvlm.md]
- 영상 이해 지원. 제출 2025-04-07.^[inbox/processed/hw-20261021-model-smolvlm.md]
