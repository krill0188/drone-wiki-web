# US Drone Import Tariffs 2026

Trump administration announced sweeping new tariffs on imported drones and drone components, with duties reaching up to 100% for certain products.

## Policy Details

- **Tariff Range**: 25-100% on imported drones and components
- **Effective**: Announced 2026-08-17
- **Target**: Boost domestic manufacturing, reduce dependence on foreign suppliers

## Impact Areas

- Consumer drone purchases
- Business and enterprise operations
- Public safety agencies
- Recreational drone pilots

## Related Developments

- DJI Pentagon blacklist challenge partially reversed by D.C. Circuit Court
- Separate US market restrictions on DJI continue
