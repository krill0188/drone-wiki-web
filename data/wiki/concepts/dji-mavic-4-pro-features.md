# DJI Mavic 4 Pro Camera and Lito X1 ActiveTrack

DJI's latest drone camera capabilities showcasing the Mavic 4 Pro's Hasselblad 100MP sensor and Lito X1's ActiveTrack for dynamic subject following.

## DJI Mavic 4 Pro

### Camera Specifications
- **Sensor**: Hasselblad 4/3 CMOS
- **Resolution**: 100MP
- **Capability**: Wide coastal frame with discoverable layers
- **Feature**: Every crop feels like a new shot

### Use Cases
- Aerial photography with detail extraction
- Professional cinematography
- Landscape and coastal documentation

## DJI Lito X1

### ActiveTrack Features
- Dynamic subject following during movement
- Hiking and outdoor activity tracking
- Maintains shot composition automatically

### Capabilities
- Keeps up with subjects in motion
- Reduces manual piloting effort
- Suitable for mountain trips and outdoor activities
