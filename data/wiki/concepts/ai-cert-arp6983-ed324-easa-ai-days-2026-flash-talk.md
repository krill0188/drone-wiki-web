- 결론 슬라이드: "EASA is considering ED-324/ARP6983 as an anticipated AMC", ANAC 등 타 당국도 인정 방향, "discussions with the FAA and other regulators continue".^[inbox/processed/hw-20261022-ai-cert-arp6983-ed324-easa-ai-days-2026-flash-talk.md]
